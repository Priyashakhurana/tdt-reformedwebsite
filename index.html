The Drip Theory is a Reddit and LinkedIn marketing agency for B2B SaaS companies. We find the problems your ideal customers post about on Reddit, answer them in the right subreddits as the founder, and turn those conversations into LinkedIn posts that build inbound pipeline.

Reddit replies can bring profile visits in the first week. LinkedIn builds month by month. Threads that rank on Google keep working long after they go up, and you see progress in every weekly report.

We follow each subreddit's rules, lead with useful answers, and only mention your product when it actually fits the question. No fake accounts, no vote manipulation.
